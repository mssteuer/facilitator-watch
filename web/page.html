How to read this profile

Declared means advertised in /supported. Payment behavior is untested. No /verify, /settle or payment requests are made.

Stale retains the last positive evidence after failure or an omission. Not advertised means recently omitted, not unsupported or withdrawn. Failures break the consecutive omission count.

Bazaar declarations do not establish discovery availability. A reachable endpoint can return an invalid declaration or HTTP error.

Evidence semantics

Raw kinds[] preserves x402Version, scheme, network and optional extra. Unknown fields remain in raw JSON. Legacy network IDs are not rewritten.

Declaration and behavior are independent evidence channels. This monitor collects declarations only. No payment, settlement or optional discovery behavior is tested. First seen is the earliest retained positive observation, not launch time.

Failure never proves removal. After three consecutive successful snapshots omit a known capability, it is marked not advertised. There is no provider withdrawal inference.
